01 The shop
No gatekeeping
ever

Old-school room.
New blood.

Lady Luck opened in Owensboro in 2007 with one rule: good tattoos, done properly, by people who actually want you in the chair.

Fifteen years later we brought the same room across the river — knotty pine, brass, low amber light, and a turquoise wall you can spot from Sand Lane. It feels a little like the inside of a ship. It acts like your friend's kitchen.

Every artist here is a woman. Every client gets the same treatment. First-timers, collectors, mother-son duos with no plan at all — pull up. We'll tell you the truth about what will age well, then draw the rest.
